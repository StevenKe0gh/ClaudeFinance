How the signal score works

Each stock gets up to 100 points from the filings in the window. Congress: up to 25 for how many different members bought, 10 for the estimated dollar amount, and 5 if they bought without selling. Insiders: up to 25 for how many different insiders made open-market purchases (Form 4 code P), 15 for the dollar amount, and 5 if a CEO, CFO, president or chair bought. Stocks bought by both groups get 15 more. Awards, option exercises and tax withholding are ignored because they are not decisions to buy.

Before you place an order

  • Check the price chart since the filing date. If the stock already ran up, much of the signal may be priced in.
  • Congress has up to 45 days to report a trade, and amounts are ranges. You are always seeing the trade late.
  • Insider purchases are the stronger signal; sales happen for taxes, diversification or pre-set plans.
  • Use limit orders, and decide now what would make you sell (a price, a date, or the insiders selling).
  • Review the plan in 30 days and refresh the signals before adding money.
