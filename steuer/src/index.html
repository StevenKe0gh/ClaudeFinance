Steuererklärung 2024–2026

Get back the wage tax withheld from your internships. As a student your yearly income stays near or below the basic allowance (Grundfreibetrag), so most or all of it is refunded. Enter each internship once; the page splits it into tax years and lists what to type into ELSTER.

Estimates for preparing your return, based on § 32a EStG for 2024–2026 and the allowances in force (Arbeitnehmer-Pauschbetrag 1.230 €, Sparer-Pauschbetrag 1.000 €, Sonderausgaben-Pauschbetrag 36 €). The Finanzamt's assessment is binding. Not tax advice: for unusual cases ask a Lohnsteuerhilfeverein or a Steuerberater. Your entries are stored privately in your account.
