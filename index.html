Mental-Math Armory

MOIC → IRR (5-yr hold):

1.5x2.0x2.5x3.0x4.0x
~8%~15%~20%~25%~32%

Rule of 72: years to double ≈ 72 ÷ rate. A 2x in 5 yrs ⇒ 72/5 ≈ 14.4% ≈ 15%.

Compounding shortcut: growth g over n years ≈ 1 + n·g + small upward nudge (e.g. 7% × 5y ≈ 1.40 vs exact 1.403).

Sequence: entry EV → debt/equity split → exit EBITDA → exit EV → subtract remaining debt → MOIC → map to IRR. Say it in that order, always.
